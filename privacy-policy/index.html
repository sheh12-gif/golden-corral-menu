Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information this website collects, why we collect it, and what choices you have. We have written it in plain language on purpose, because a policy nobody can follow is not much use to anyone.

This policy applies to goldencorralcost.us and to every page published under that domain. It does not apply to any other website, including the official Golden Corral website.

Information We Collect

Information You Give Us

We receive personal information only when you choose to send it. That usually means your email address and the contents of your message when you write to us. We do not run a contact form that stores data on this site, and you never need an account to read anything here.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when a page is requested. This typically includes the page you visited, the date and time, your browser and device type, and a general geographic area derived from your IP address. This information is used to keep the site running and to understand which pages are useful.

How We Use Information

  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To keep the site secure and available.
  • To understand which pages and topics readers find most useful.
  • To display advertising that keeps the site free to read.
  • To meet legal obligations where they apply.

We do not sell personal information. We do not rent email addresses. We do not build profiles of individual readers for our own marketing.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. They help a site remember your preferences, measure how pages perform, and support advertising. We also use similar technologies such as local storage and pixels.

Some cookies are set by us and some are set by third parties whose services appear on the site. Each category is described in our cookie policy.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This website is supported by advertising. We use Google AdSense to display ads, which allows us to keep every guide free to read. Advertising is the only reason this site can exist without a paywall or a subscription.

Third Party Vendors

Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and to other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and to other sites on the internet.

The DoubleClick Cookie

Google uses the DoubleClick cookie, along with other identifiers, to serve ads and to measure how those ads perform. The cookie may record which pages you have viewed so that advertising is more relevant to you. Data collected for advertising is handled under Google's own privacy terms.

Personalized Advertising and Opting Out

You can turn off personalized advertising from Google by opening Google Ads Settings and adjusting your preferences. When personalized advertising is switched off you may still see ads, but they will be based on general factors such as your approximate location and the content of the page rather than on your browsing history.

Google publishes full details of its advertising practices and the rules that publishers must follow. You can read Google advertising technologies for an explanation of how advertising cookies work, and the Google AdSense Program policies for the standards that publishers are held to.

Analytics

We may use an analytics service to understand how readers move through the site. Analytics tools record information such as pages viewed, time on page, the referring website, browser type, and approximate location. This information is aggregated and is not used to identify you by name.

Where an analytics service supports it, we enable IP anonymisation so that the final portion of your IP address is removed before it is stored.

Links to Other Websites

Our pages link to other websites, including restaurant locations, ordering pages, and policy pages. We do not control those sites and we are not responsible for their content or their privacy practices. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of any site you visit.

How Long We Keep Information

Emails you send us are kept only for as long as they are useful, which is usually the period needed to answer your question and to track corrections. Technical logs held by our hosting provider are kept according to their standard retention periods.

Children's Privacy

This website is written for adults and is not directed at children under thirteen. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Your Rights and Choices

Visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom

If you are in these regions, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, to object to certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Visitors in California

California residents may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

Controlling Cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Doing so may affect how parts of this site work. Every major browser provides a privacy or security menu where cookies can be managed.

Changes to This Policy

We review this policy from time to time and update it when our practices change or when the law requires it. The effective date shown on this page will always reflect the most recent version. Continuing to use the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

How to Contact Us
