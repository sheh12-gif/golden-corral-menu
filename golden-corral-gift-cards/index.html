Golden Corral has been serving endless buffet favorites since 1973, and its gift cards are one of the easiest ways to treat someone to breakfast, lunch or dinner. The good news is they are simple to buy and they work at hundreds of locations across the country. You just need to know which place to buy from to get full value and avoid a bad card.

Buy on the official site

Buying from the official site is the safest option. You can order a golden corral gift card online in under two minutes. Choose who it is for, add a personal message, pick your amount and select email or mail. The digital version lands in your inbox quickly and includes your card number and PIN. You can even schedule it for a future date.

Golden Corral gift cards at Walmart

Yes, you can find golden corral gift cards at Walmart. Online you will see the $25 physical card sold and shipped by Walmart.com. In store, look in the gift card mall near the checkout lanes. Availability changes by location, so call ahead if you need one today. The Walmart listing has no extra fees and shows strong ratings from buyers.

Golden Corral gift card Costco deal

Costco is a favorite for families who visit often. The warehouse sells a pack of four $25 cards for $79.99. That is $20.01 in savings, which equals 20 percent off. During the holidays it has dropped to $74.99. You need a Costco membership and the cards are mailed, so order a week ahead. Executive members earn 2 percent back even on gift cards.

Golden Corral gift cards Sam's Club deal

If you want everyday savings, Sam's Club is hard to beat. The club sells golden corral gift cards Sam's Club members love, the two pack of $25 cards for $39.98. You save $10.02 on every $50 you buy. It is available all year with free shipping. Sam's Club also lists a single $25 card for $23.98. Around Black Friday the two pack has been as low as $34.98.

Quick tip: If you need a gift tonight, use the official digital card. If you can wait a few days and have a membership, Sam's Club and Costco give you instant savings without any coupon.

Stay safe: Golden Corral only sells gift cards at its restaurants and at goldencorral.com/giftcards. If you see one at a pawn shop, flea market or on social media, it may not be valid. Keep your receipt until the balance is zero.

How to Get a Golden Corral Gift Cards Discount Without Risk

There are two legit ways to pay less. One is seasonal and one is year round.

  • Holiday bonus: From mid November through early January, Golden Corral often runs buy $50 get $10 free. You must buy in the restaurant or on the official site or app, and the bonus is usually valid in January and February.
  • Warehouse packs: Costco $100 for $79.99 and Sam's Club $50 for $39.98 are available all year. No promo code needed.

Sites that promise 24 percent off from resellers sound tempting, but they carry real risk. Buyers have reported invalid cards with no refund. For a gift, peace of mind is worth more than a tiny extra discount. If you love hunting for seasonal savings, you will also want to check our guide to golden corral promos and discounts for current coupons, bundle meals and limited time buffet specials.

Checking your golden corral gift cards balance takes less than a minute. Pick the method that is easiest for you.

The phone numbers printed on cards include 888 892 3813 and 844 458 4706 depending on where the card was issued, so use the one on your card. If your PIN is scratched off or hard to read, take the card to the restaurant. The register can often pull the balance with a swipe. Your receipt after a meal also shows the remaining balance. Digital cards have the number and PIN inside the email, so screenshot that email when it arrives.

How to Use Your Card

You can use your card for dine in, takeout and to go meals. Many locations also let you apply it to online orders placed through the official website for pickup. It covers the buffet, drinks, tax and even the tip, up to your available balance. If your total is higher, just pay the rest with cash or another card.

A couple of things to know. You cannot use a gift card to buy another gift card. You do not earn rewards points when you buy a gift card, but you do earn points when you pay for your meal with one. And if the restaurant internet is down, validation can fail, so keep a backup payment method with you just in case.

Final Thoughts

Golden corral gift cards make gifting easy because they work for everyone, never expire and cover the whole meal. If you need it now, buy digital on the official site. If you want to save, grab the warehouse packs and keep an eye out for the holiday bonus.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I purchase a Golden Corral gift card? +
You can buy at any Golden Corral restaurant and on the official site for $5 to $50 as a physical or digital card. Trusted retailers include Walmart for a $25 card, Costco with a four pack for $79.99, Sam's Club with a two pack for $39.98, plus Kroger, Amazon and Giftcards.com for $15 to $250.
How long is a Golden Corral gift card good for? +
It does not expire and has no fees. You can use it whenever you are ready. This applies to all standard gift cards. Promo bonus cards do expire and will show their own dates.
Can you buy Golden Corral gift cards at Walmart? +
Yes. Walmart sells a $25 Golden Corral gift card online that ships to your home. In stores, check the gift card mall near checkout. Stock varies by location.
Where to buy Golden Corral gift cards for the best deal? +
For everyday savings, Sam's Club at $39.98 for $50 is the best regular price. For holiday gifting, the official buy $50 get $10 bonus gives you extra value and supports the restaurant directly.
Does Dollar General sell Golden Corral gift cards? +
No. Dollar General does not carry Golden Corral gift cards. Its gift card rack is in store only and does not list Golden Corral. Stick to the official site, Walmart, Costco or Sam's Club. Avoid pawn shops and social media sellers.
Can I use a Golden Corral gift card online? +
Yes, for to go and pickup orders placed on the official site or app at participating locations. It may not work on third party delivery apps.
Can I reload my Golden Corral gift card? +
No. Current cards cannot be reloaded. When your balance reaches zero, simply buy a new card.
