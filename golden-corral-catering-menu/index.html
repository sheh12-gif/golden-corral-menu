Golden Corral Catering Menu: Prices, Packages & How to Order

Feeding a crowd should not require a spreadsheet and three phone calls. The Golden Corral catering menu keeps it simple with party platters, boxed meals and side trays built for 10 to 100 people, and most events land between $6 and $14 per guest. What follows is current pricing, honest serving sizes, holiday cutoffs and the fastest path to placing an order.

One thing to clear up early. Catering here means food in trays and boxes, not a staffed setup. Nobody arrives with chafing dishes, plates or servers. If your venue needs hot food held for hours, you will want to rent warmers or plan delivery close to serve time.

Not every Golden Corral caters, either. Of roughly 350 restaurants, about 253 run a catering storefront. Put your ZIP code in first and confirm before you build a menu around it.

Golden Corral Catering Prices in 2026

Here is where expectations need adjusting. There is no single national price list, and anyone who hands you one is guessing.

Nearly every Golden Corral is independently owned, so each restaurant sets its own tray prices, portion sizes and menu mix. The golden corral catering price you see at checkout is the only one that counts for your event.

The gap between stores is real and sometimes large.

How to Order Golden Corral Catering

Ordering takes about ten minutes if you already know your headcount.

  1. Open the official catering site and enter your city, state or ZIP code.
  2. Choose the nearest catering storefront. Skip restaurants that do not show up, since they do not cater.
  3. Select pickup or delivery, then a date and time slot.
  4. Build the order with boxed meals for a headcount or platters and trays for a buffet line.
  5. For each boxed meal, pick two sides from that store's list.
  6. Review the cart total, which is the real price with tax, then check out.

You can also call the restaurant directly if you need something unusual, like 150 guests or a mixed order for a wedding. Walking in and talking to a manager still works best for the biggest events.

Booking ahead matters more than people expect. Pickup slots follow each restaurant's Golden Corral hours, so the calendar will only show times the kitchen can actually handle.

The 72 hour rule is an official requirement for holiday feasts. For everyday catering, treat 24 to 48 hours as your safety window. Popular stores fill up on Fridays and weekends first.

Delivery is the biggest wildcard. Some stores run their own drivers and some do not deliver at all. Ask when you call, or check whether the delivery tab is live before you choose a time.

One more detail worth knowing: catering orders do not earn or redeem Golden Corral Rewards points. A loyalty program for catering is in the works, but it is not here yet. Do not count on points to offset the bill.

Each feast includes the protein, mashed potatoes, gravy, stuffing, cranberry sauce, a dozen yeast rolls and a whole pie. You reheat and serve, which is a fair trade for not wrestling a turkey at six in the morning.

Dates that matter this season:

  • Pickup begins November 2, 2026.
  • Orders need 72 hours of notice, minimum.
  • Rewards members get $10 off feasts ordered in the app between October 5 and November 1.
  • Thanksgiving falls on Thursday, November 26, 2026.
  • Christmas Eve orders should be placed by roughly December 21.

Holiday meals do not work through third party delivery apps, so plan on picking yours up. If cooking at home is not the goal at all, most restaurants stay open on Thanksgiving Day with a holiday buffet for about $17.99 to $21.99 per adult. Just confirm locally, since each store sets its own hours.

The golden corral to go menu is a separate ordering system from catering, and the two do not mix in one cart. To go works well for a family dinner on a Tuesday. It stops making sense around 20 people, because you end up buying 20 individual meals instead of three platters.

If your group would rather sit down and let someone else handle the dishes, compare Golden Corral buffet options first. Every location also has a free private party room, which is an option most people forget to ask about.

Ways to Save on a Catering Order

Catering is already one of the cheapest ways to feed a crowd comfort food, and a few habits push the bill lower.

  • Build from side trays. A $25 pan of macaroni and cheese feeds more people per dollar than any entrée.
  • Skip individual drinks and buy gallon containers instead. It saves roughly 30 percent on beverages.
  • Order desserts by the tray or cake, never by the piece.
  • Pick up at an off peak time, like 2 PM instead of noon, and you avoid staffing delays.
  • Check Golden Corral coupons before checkout, since seasonal offers occasionally include catering.

One warning on timing. Festive weeks sell out first, and a store that looks calm in early November will not have your slot on the twenty fourth.

The Bottom Line

The golden corral catering menu is one of the most affordable ways to feed a group real comfort food, as long as you build it for your headcount and price it at your own store. Trays for 15 or more, boxed meals for smaller teams, and 72 hours of breathing room for the holidays will keep you out of trouble. Start with your ZIP code, look at the real cart total, and order confident.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Golden Corral offer catering? +
Yes. Participating restaurants sell large party platters, individually boxed meals, side trays, salads and desserts. Pickup is available at every catering location and delivery at many.
How much does Golden Corral catering cost? +
Plan on $6 to $14 per guest. Boxed meals run $11.99 to $13.99 per person, entrée platters $24.99 to $55.99, and side trays $14.99 to $35.00.
How far in advance should I order? +
Holiday feasts require 72 hours. Everyday catering is safest with 24 to 48 hours of notice, and events over 70 people need 3 to 7 days.
Is there a minimum order? +
It varies by store. One location required a $50 food minimum plus a $20 delivery fee, so ask when you call.
Does catering include delivery? +
Many locations offer it, but fees and minimums are set by each restaurant. Pickup is always an option.
What comes with an individually packaged meal? +
One entrée, your choice of two sides, a yeast roll, honey butter and cookies.
How many people does one tray feed? +
An entrée platter feeds about 10, a side tray about 15, and a salad about 10. Cakes serve 14 and pies serve 10.
Can I order breakfast catering? +
Only at select locations. A small number of stores offer morning trays, so confirm before you plan a breakfast meeting.
Do catering orders earn Rewards points? +
No. Catering currently earns and redeems nothing under Golden Corral Rewards.
Can I customize a platter? +
Entrées are fixed. Some stores allow side or salad swaps, so ask while ordering.
Is catering cheaper than eating in? +
For 15 or more people, yes, usually by a lot. A tray built meal often lands near $7 per person against $12 or more for boxed meals.
Does Golden Corral cater Thanksgiving and Christmas? +
Yes. Holiday Meals To-Go serve 6 to 8, include sides, a dozen rolls and a whole pie, and run $169.99 to $199.99 in 2026 with 72 hours of notice.
