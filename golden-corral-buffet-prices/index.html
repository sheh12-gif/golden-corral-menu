Golden Corral Buffet Prices: Cost Per Person for Every Meal

Figuring out what a meal at Golden Corral will actually cost should not take three phone calls and a forum search. Everything you need is right here: Golden Corral buffet prices for adults, seniors and kids, plus the drink, tax and tip costs most price lists quietly skip. Read this once and you will walk in knowing your total before you reach the register.

Golden Corral Buffet Prices Per Person

The system is simple. You pay one price at the register, then eat as much as you want from every station in the building. That single number changes with the meal period and the age of the diner.

Meal Best time to go Adult Senior (60+) Kids 4 to 8 Kids 9 to 12
Breakfast Saturday and Sunday mornings $12.99 $12.49 $8.99 $9.99
Lunch Monday to Friday, 11 AM to 4 PM $11.49 $10.99 $8.99 $9.99
Dinner Daily, from 4 PM $16.99 $15.99 $10.49 $11.49

Treat those figures as what most people pay rather than a national rate card. Every location is set up as its own franchise, so your store decides its own numbers. Drinks also sit outside the base price, which adds roughly twenty percent to a ticket. Both points get unpacked as you read.

Golden Corral Buffet Prices for Adults

Anyone between 13 and 59 pays the standard adult rate. Lunch is the cheapest ticket of the day and dinner is the most expensive, and there is a real reason for that gap. The dinner line brings out the signature sirloin steak, the carving station and the shrimp. Lunch does not.

Meal Days Time Typical adult price
Breakfast Saturday and Sunday at most stores 7:30 AM to 11 AM $12.99 (range $10.99 to $14.49)
Lunch Monday to Friday 11 AM to 4 PM $11.49 to $12.99
Dinner Monday to Friday 4 PM to close $16.99 to $17.99
Dinner Saturday and Sunday Noon to close $16.99 to $19.99

The jump from lunch to dinner is about five dollars at most stores. If your plate is going to be fried chicken, pot roast, mac and cheese and a loaded salad, lunch hands you almost the same meal for noticeably less. If steak is the whole reason you are going, dinner is when it comes out.

Once a guest turns 13, they are charged as an adult. There is no teen tier, so a family with older kids should budget accordingly.

Golden Corral Buffet Prices for Seniors

Guests 60 and over usually pay a dollar or two less than the adult rate at every meal. Nothing needs clipping, no card needs signing and no app needs downloading. You simply have to ask the cashier for the senior price when you pay, because it rarely appears on the board.

Meal Days Time Senior price What you save
Breakfast Saturday and Sunday 9 AM to noon $11.99 to $13.99 About $1
Lunch Monday to Friday 11 AM to 4 PM $10.99 to $12.99 $1 to $1.50
Dinner Monday to Friday 4 PM to close $15.99 to $16.99 About $1.50
Dinner Saturday and Sunday Noon to close $15.99 to $17.99 $1.50 to $2
Early bird Monday to Friday Commonly 2 PM to 4 PM $9.99 to $10.99 Drink often included

Ask before the cashier rings you up. Once the ticket closes, changing it becomes a hassle for everyone.

Sixty is the cutoff at most stores, though a smaller number use 55 or 65 because the owner of each restaurant decides. Bringing a photo ID is sensible even though many locations never ask.

The early bird is the best deal in the building. A senior paying $10.99 at 1:45 PM on a Tuesday, with a fountain drink thrown in, can sit next to someone who arrives at 4:05 PM and pays dinner pricing for the exact same food. That is often six or seven dollars saved per person.

Kids' Prices and Age Groups

Children are priced by age, and the youngest ones eat free. That policy is the reason so many families pick this place on a Sunday.

9 to 12 $9.99 $11.49 One step below adult pricing
13 and up Adult price Adult price Charged as an adult

The free child rule is generally capped at two kids per paying adult, so four little ones and two adults means paying for two of them. Drink inclusion varies by store, so ask at the register rather than assuming.

Golden Corral Breakfast Buffet

The Golden Corral breakfast buffet price is typically $12.99 for adults, with a realistic range of $10.99 to $14.49 depending on the store. Seniors usually pay around $12.49 and kids land between $8.99 and $9.99.

Breakfast Hours

Most locations only serve breakfast on Saturday and Sunday. A minority open for breakfast on weekdays too, and those stores vary widely, with some starting at 7 AM and others at 8 AM.

Day Breakfast served Typical window
Monday to Thursday At some locations only 7:30 AM to 11 AM
Friday At some locations only 7:30 AM to 11 AM
Saturday At most locations 7 AM to 11 AM, sometimes 11:30
Sunday At most locations 7 AM to 11 AM, sometimes 11:30

Breakfast wraps up at 11 AM almost everywhere, and busy weekend stores stretch that to 11:30. There is no all day breakfast here. Show up at noon expecting eggs and you will find the lunch line instead. Arriving around 10:45 catches the tail end of breakfast and the first of lunch in one visit, since the trays swap over gradually.

Golden Corral Lunch Buffet

The Golden Corral lunch buffet price runs $11.49 to $12.99 for adults on weekdays. Seniors pay $10.99 to $12.99. This is the cheapest window for a full hot meal, and for most people it is the smartest time to visit.

Lunch at a glance Details

Hours Monday to Friday, 11 AM to 4 PM

The price you pay is set when you reach the register, not when you sit down. Getting in line at 3:50 PM locks in lunch pricing. Arriving at 4:05 PM costs you the dinner rate for food that looks nearly identical on the plate.

Golden Corral Dinner Buffet

The Golden Corral dinner buffet price is $16.99 to $17.99 on weekdays and $16.99 to $19.99 on weekends. It is the most expensive ticket of the day, and it is also the one that gives you the most food for the money.

Dinner Hours

Day Dinner window
Monday to Thursday 4 PM to 9 PM
Friday and Saturday 4 PM to 10 PM at most stores
Sunday 4 PM to 9 PM

Holiday hours move, so check your location on the day you plan to go.

Dinner Menu Station

  • Carving station: Carved bone in turkey, sliced to order
  • Sirloin steak: Signature sirloin grilled the way you like it, at no extra charge
  • Seafood: Golden Delicious Shrimp
  • Hot favorites: Bourbon Street Chicken, Awesome Pot Roast, Meatloaf, Fried Chicken, Fried Fish, Baked Fish, pot pie or dumplings, pizzas, a rotating pork entree
  • Hot sides: The full lunch lineup plus cinnamon apples and sweet corn pudding
  • Salad bar: The same fresh spread you get at lunch
  • Desserts: The same dessert station, with chocolate dipped strawberry, Rice Krispies and marshmallow skewers

Three items change the value math completely, and they are the reason dinner costs more. Sirloin steak grilled to order, a carving station with bone in turkey, and shrimp. A single steak dinner elsewhere usually costs more than this entire buffet, which is why the five dollar upgrade from lunch looks reasonable to anyone who eats beef.

Weekend dinners run a dollar or two higher than weeknights. That premium buys a broader spread and often some seasonal extras.

Drinks, Tax and Tip: The Real Cost of a Visit

The prices above are not your final bill. Drinks, sales tax and an optional tip all sit on top, and missing that is how people get surprised at the register.

To Go and Weigh and Pay Prices

Not everyone wants to sit down for an hour. The to go option lets you fill a container from the buffet and pay by weight at the register.

Meal period Price per pound
Breakfast $6.99 to $8.99
Lunch $6.99 to $9.99
Dinner $8.99 to $10.99

This is a different deal from the endless buffet. You pay for what you take rather than for unlimited access, so it rewards a light eater and punishes anyone who piles the box high. For a quick lunch it is hard to beat.

Golden Corral Buffet Prices Near Me: Why the Numbers Move

Two people in different states can pay very different amounts for the same tray of food. Golden Corral buffet prices near me searches turn up a dozen different answers for a simple reason: there is no single national price.

Five things move the number:

  • The meal period: Breakfast is cheapest, lunch is in the middle, dinner is highest.
  • The day of the week: Weekends carry dinner pricing from midday at many stores.
  • The age of the diner: Under 3 is free, then two kids tiers, then adult, with a senior rate on top.
  • The location: High cost metro areas in states like California and New York sit at the top of every range.
  • The calendar: Thanksgiving, Christmas, Easter and Mother's Day can bring special menus and premium pricing.

Getting your real number takes about a minute. Open the store locator on the official Golden Corral site, enter your zip code, and open your nearest restaurant. The store page lists current hours and the full breakfast, lunch and dinner menus for that specific location.

Then call and ask three questions before you go:

  • What is the adult buffet price today?
  • Is the senior price available right now?
  • Does that include a drink?

Those three answers remove every guess from your budget.

The Golden Corral Rewards program is worth joining before your first visit. Points build at one per dollar, and a hundred points turns into ten dollars off. The sign up bonus alone covers most of a kid's meal, and points keep earning even when you use a senior rate.

A few habits that quietly cut the bill:

  • Ask for the senior rate and ask about early bird separately. They are not the same thing.
  • Skip the paid drink if you are counting pennies. Water is free.
  • Go Monday through Thursday, when the weekend premium does not apply.
  • Bring cash for a tip, because not every register makes it easy to add one.
  • Check your store's page on the day you visit, especially around holidays.

Final Thoughts

Golden Corral buffet prices come down to three choices: which meal you pick, who is eating, and which store you walk into. Plan for lunch if you want the best value, ask about the senior rate if someone at the table qualifies, and budget for the drink and tip. Get those right and there will be no surprises at the register.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the Golden Corral buffet per person? +
Most adults pay about $11.49 to $12.99 at weekday lunch and $16.99 to $19.99 at dinner. Seniors pay a dollar or two less, and kids pay between $8.99 and $11.49 by age.
What time is the cheapest time to eat at Golden Corral? +
Weekday lunch, Monday to Friday from 11 AM to 4 PM, is the cheapest standard window at about $11.49 to $12.99. Guests 60 and over pay less during the weekday early bird, often $9.99 to $10.99 with a drink included.
What does it cost for seniors to eat at Golden Corral? +
Most seniors pay about $10.99 for weekday lunch, $12.49 for weekend breakfast and $15.99 for dinner. The weekday early bird brings that down to roughly $9.99 to $10.99.
What time is senior prices at Golden Corral? +
Most locations honor the senior rate all day. Many also run a weekday early bird, commonly between 2 PM and 4 PM, though some start as early as 11 AM.
Do you leave a tip at Golden Corral? +
Tipping is optional and no service charge is added. About ten percent, or one to three dollars per person, is the usual gesture for drink refills and cleared plates.
Are drinks included in the buffet price? +
Not at lunch or dinner. Fountain drinks run about $3.49 to $3.99 with free refills. Some locations include a beverage with weekend breakfast.
Does Golden Corral serve breakfast every day? +
Most locations serve breakfast on Saturday and Sunday only. A smaller number open for breakfast on weekdays, usually starting between 7 and 8 AM.
Is steak included in the buffet? +
Yes, the signature sirloin steak is part of the dinner buffet at no extra charge and is grilled to order. It is not on the lunch line, which is why dinner costs more.
Can kids eat free at Golden Corral? +
Children 3 and under eat free with a paying adult, usually limited to two free kids per adult. Kids aged 4 to 12 pay a reduced rate based on age.
Do prices change on weekends and holidays? +
Yes. Weekend dinners typically run a dollar or two higher, and some stores move to dinner pricing from midday on Saturday and Sunday. Holidays can carry special menus and premium prices.
Do I need a reservation? +
No. Most locations are walk in only. For a large group, call ahead and ask about the private party room, since every location has one.
Is there a military discount? +
Many locations offer roughly 10 to 15 percent off with a valid military ID, set store by store. Golden Corral also holds an annual Military Appreciation Night around Veterans Day with a free meal for service members and veterans.
