Ways to Save on Dinner

You do not have to pay full price every visit. A few programs make a real difference, especially for families and seniors.

  • Good as Gold Rewards App. Earn 1 point per dollar and get $10 after 100 points. New members often get $5 off. You also get birthday rewards and app only coupons.
  • Good as Gold Email Club. Separate from the app. You get seasonal coupons and birthday offers by email. Many guests join both to get the most savings.
  • Senior Early Bird. Guests 60 and older can often get a special from about $9.99 between 11 am and 3 pm on weekdays at participating places. Regular senior dinner remains $13.99 to $17.99.
  • Kids Eat Free. Kids 3 and under eat free with a paying adult for dine in. Kids 4 to 12 have reduced tickets and drinks are often included.
  • Holiday Gift Card Bonus. During holidays you can buy $50 in gift cards and get a $10 bonus card for later use at many locations.
  • Military Appreciation. Most restaurants offer a military discount and every Veterans Day on Nov 11 they host a free dinner for active duty, retirees, reservists and veterans from 4 pm to close for dine in.

You will also find group party rooms and catering platters for pickup if you are planning a larger gathering. The morning breakfast service that runs on weekends has a separate selection and is a nice option if you prefer an earlier visit.

Final Thoughts

The golden corral dinner menu gives you the widest choice of the day at a ticket that still feels fair for families when you plan around hours and deals. Pick a weekday for the lowest price, arrive right at 4 pm for the freshest steak and carving, and use the rewards app to save on your next visit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is Golden Corral dinner for adults +
Adult dinner is typically $16.99 to $17.99 on weekdays and $17.99 to $19.99 on weekends in 2026. Seniors pay a little less, kids 4 to 12 have discounted tickets, kids 3 and under are free with an adult, and drinks are extra. Your local restaurant sets the exact price so confirm before you go.
Is Golden Corral free on your birthday +
Not automatically, but you can get a reward through the Good as Gold Club, often called the Birthday Club. Join for free on the app or website or text JOIN to 75272 at least two weeks before your birthday and add your birthdate. During your birthday month you will usually get either a buy one adult ticket get one free coupon or a free ticket at many locations. It is valid once, for dine in only, and cannot be combined with other offers. Some places may ask for photo ID, and offers vary by franchise.
What time is the dinner menu at Golden Corral +
Dinner starts at 4 pm daily. Most places close at 9 pm Monday to Thursday and 9 pm to 10 pm Friday to Sunday. If you pay before 4 pm you get the lunch price even if you stay into dinner hours.
What is the difference between lunch and dinner at Golden Corral +
Dinner keeps everything from lunch and adds the sirloin steak cooked to order, the carving station and an expanded seafood rotation. That is why dinner costs about $5 to $6 more. The salad bar, sides and dessert bar are largely the same at both meals.
