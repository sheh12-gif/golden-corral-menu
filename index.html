❓ Frequently Asked Questions About Golden Corral Prices

Everything you need to know before visiting Golden Corral in October 2026.

How much is Golden Corral buffet per person in October 2026? +
As of October 2026, an adult buffet at Golden Corral costs $12.49 for weekday lunch, $17.49 for weekday dinner (Mon–Thu), $18.49 for weekend dinner (Fri–Sun), and $12.99 for weekend breakfast. Children aged 3 and under always eat free with a paying adult.
What time does Golden Corral switch from lunch to dinner prices? +
Golden Corral switches from lunch pricing to dinner buffet pricing at 4:00 PM Monday through Saturday. On Sundays, most locations transition directly from the morning breakfast buffet into all-day Sunday dinner pricing at 11:00 AM.
How much is Golden Corral Weigh & Pay per pound in 2026? +
Golden Corral's To-Go Weigh & Pay costs $6.99 per pound during weekday lunch hours (before 4:00 PM) and $8.99 per pound during dinner hours and weekends. If you include signature sirloin steak or jumbo shrimp in your takeout box, a $2.00/lb premium upgrade applies.
Are drinks included in the Golden Corral buffet price? +
Standard adult and kid buffet prices do not include beverages; fountain soft drinks and iced tea cost $2.99 extra (with free refills), while coffee is $2.59. However, seniors aged 60+ who visit during the Early Bird Special (Mon–Fri, 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM) receive their drink included for $10.99.
Does Golden Corral offer a senior or military discount in 2026? +
Yes! Seniors aged 60 and older automatically save $1.00 off regular adult buffet rates all day, plus enjoy the $10.99 Early Bird special on weekday afternoons. Active-duty military, veterans, and uniformed first responders receive 10% to 15% off with a valid ID.
