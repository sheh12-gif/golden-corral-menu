What's on the Golden Corral Buffet Menu

The buffet has almost 150 items, and Golden Corral says most are made from scratch throughout the day. Dishes rotate by day, season, and location, so you'll often find something new. You can see the full lineup on the official buffet menu.

Breakfast Buffet

Breakfast is mostly a weekend treat, served at select franchise locations. The Golden Corral morning buffet usually includes:

  • Made to order omelets with ham, bacon, sausage, peppers, mushrooms, onions, and cheddar
  • Scrambled eggs, bacon, sausage links and patties
  • Biscuits with sausage gravy, corned beef hash, and carved ham
  • Pancakes, French toast, Golden Waffles, and cinnamon rolls
  • Hash browns, breakfast casserole, and Texas toast
  • Cereal, yogurt, donuts, fresh fruit, juice, and milk

Lunch Buffet

Golden Corral lunch prices get you a huge spread of homestyle comfort food. Expect favorites like these:

  • Steakburger Bar, Bourbon Street Chicken, and Awesome Pot Roast
  • Fried chicken, meatloaf, fried fish, and baked fish
  • Pot pie or chicken and noodle dumplings, plus fresh pizza
  • Soup of the day, the salad bar, and a baked potato bar
  • Sides like mac and cheese, mashed potatoes, sweet corn pudding, and Mexican rice
  • Warm yeast rolls with honey butter

Dinner Buffet

The golden corral dinner menu keeps every lunch favorite and adds the premium stuff. That's why the golden corral dinner price runs higher. After 4 PM on weekdays, look for:

  • Signature Sirloin Steak, grilled to order
  • A carving station with carved bone in turkey
  • Golden Delicious Shrimp and rotating seafood specials
  • Limited time items, like the All You Can Eat Wings running this fall

Dessert Bar

Honestly, many people save the most room for this part. The dessert bar is included with every buffet and features:

  • The famous carrot cake, cupcakes, cookies, and coconut macaroons
  • Banana pudding, bread pudding, fruit cobbler, and fudge brownies
  • Soft serve ice cream with hot fudge, candy toppings, and cones
  • Chocolate dipped strawberry and marshmallow skewers
  • Cotton candy and sugar free options

Golden Corral Senior Discount

There's no single discount that works at every store. Instead, most locations set a lower senior price for guests 60 and older at every meal, usually $1 to $2 below the adult rate. Golden Corral senior buffet prices per person are listed in the price table above, and these tips help you get the most out of them:

  • Ask for it: The senior price isn't always posted on the menu board, so mention your age at the register.
  • Try the Senior Early Bird: Many stores run it on weekday afternoons, often somewhere between 11 AM and 4 PM, and it frequently includes a drink.
  • Bring an ID: Most stores take your word for it, but some may ask.
  • Check the age rule: Sixty is the most common age, but a few locations use 55 or 65.

To Go, Weigh & Pay, Delivery, and Catering

Can't stay and eat? You've got plenty of ways to enjoy the food at home:

  • Weigh & Pay: Fill a container from the buffet and pay by the pound. Expect about $6.99 to $10.99 per pound, depending on the meal and location.
  • Online pickup: Order individual meals, combos, and family meals through the app or Golden Corral online ordering.
  • Delivery: Most stores deliver through apps like DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub. Prices on delivery apps can be higher than in store.
  • Catering: Party platters of pot roast, fried chicken, mac and cheese, mashed potatoes, and more for office parties and family events.
  • Party room: Every location has a private dining room you can book for birthdays, reunions, and group meals.

Diet Friendly Options and Allergens

With so many dishes, it's easy to stick to your eating plan. Golden Corral says its buffet works for low carb, keto, vegetarian, vegan, paleo, gluten free, Mediterranean, DASH, and Weight Watchers diets.

  • Build a lighter plate: Start with the salad bar, steamed broccoli, green beans, grilled sirloin, and baked fish.
  • Check the numbers: The official nutrition page lets you search calories, sodium, and allergens for each item.
  • Know the risks: Food is served buffet style with shared utensils, so cross contact with allergens is possible.
  • Halal and kosher: Golden Corral doesn't advertise halal or kosher certified meat.

Golden Corral Locations

Golden Corral runs more than 350 restaurants across 38 states and Puerto Rico. The chain started in Fayetteville, North Carolina, in 1973 and is now based in Raleigh. A few stores closed in 2026, but the company says several will likely reopen under new owners. Six new restaurants are also in the works in Tennessee, Florida, Texas, Alabama, and Puerto Rico.

Frequently Asked Questions About Golden Corral Prices

How much is Golden Corral for adults? +
Most adults pay about $11.49 to $14.99 for lunch and $15.99 to $19.99 for dinner. Drinks cost about $3 more.
Is Golden Corral open on Christmas Day? +
It depends on the store, because Golden Corral lets each restaurant set its own Christmas Eve and Christmas Day hours, and many close. Christmas falls on Friday, December 25, 2026, so call your local store first.
How much is Golden Corral per person? +
Expect to pay about $10 to $20 per person, depending on the meal, the day, and your age. Kids 3 and under eat free with a paying adult.
Is Golden Corral open on Thanksgiving Day? +
Yes. In 2025, every Golden Corral opened from 11 AM to 4 PM on Thanksgiving, with longer hours in some areas, so check your store's hours for November 26, 2026.
What time does Golden Corral close? +
Most stores close at 9 PM Sunday through Thursday and at 10 PM on Friday and Saturday. Hours vary by location.
What are Golden Corral's new location formats? +
Golden Corral is building smaller restaurants of about 6,500 to 9,200 square feet, compared with its usual 9,000 to 11,000. It's also opening stores inside former Red Lobster, TGI Fridays, Hooters, and Party City buildings, and in travel centers.
What time is the cheapest to eat at Golden Corral? +
Weekday lunch before 4 PM is the cheapest time for adults. Seniors get the best deal with the weekday afternoon Early Bird special.
How much is it for seniors to eat at Golden Corral? +
Seniors 60 and older usually pay $10.49 to $13.99 for lunch and $14.99 to $18.99 for dinner. The Early Bird special starts at about $9.99.
Is Golden Corral lunch cheaper than dinner? +
Yes, lunch usually costs $4 to $5 less per adult. Dinner costs more because it adds sirloin steak, the carving station, and shrimp.
Is there a time limit at Golden Corral? +
No, Golden Corral has no official time limit. You can take your time and go back for seconds and thirds.
Are drinks included in the buffet price? +
Usually not, since a fountain drink costs about $2.99 to $3.49 with free refills. Some kids' prices and Senior Early Bird deals include a drink.
Does Golden Corral serve steak at lunch? +
No, the Signature Sirloin Steak is a dinner item. It shows up when dinner starts at 4 PM on weekdays.
Does Golden Corral serve breakfast every day? +
Most stores serve breakfast only on Saturday and Sunday, and only some locations offer it at all. Check your local store before you go.

Conclusion

The Golden Corral menu has something for every budget, from a $2.99 piece of fried chicken to family meals that feed six. Buffet prices run from about $11.49 at lunch to $19.99 at weekend dinner, and seniors and kids pay less. Since golden corral prices change by location, check your local store and the rewards app before you go.
