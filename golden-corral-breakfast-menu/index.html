Golden Corral breakfast is one of the better value mornings in American dining, but only if you know when to show up. Most locations run it on weekends alone, and the price changes from town to town. Here is the full Golden Corral breakfast menu, what it costs, and how to avoid a wasted drive.

Does Golden Corral Have a Breakfast Menu?

Yes, and it comes in two forms. The main event is the endless buffet, which you'll find at any store serving breakfast. Many locations also post a made to order list with individual prices, handy when you want a single biscuit and coffee rather than a full spread.

What Does a Breakfast Buffet Consist Of?

Five stations, and the omelet counter is the one worth queuing for.

The core line holds steady, but the edges move. Southern and Southwestern markets often add chorizo and eggs, migas or a breakfast pizza, while cheesy hashbrown casserole turns up in some stores and not others. Treat anything beyond the standard list as a bonus.

How Much Does Golden Corral Charge for Breakfast Per Person?

No national price board exists, because nearly every store is franchised and each owner sets their own. Four things decide your total: the meal period, the day, the age group and local running costs. Breakfast is always the cheapest ticket, with prices climbing through lunch before peaking at dinner.

A family of four shows the spread nicely. Two adults at $12, a 10 year old at $10 and a 6 year old at $8 comes to about $42 before drinks. Smaller markets land nearer $37, busy metros push past $47. Worth checking the official store locator before you go, since a store opening at 10:30 AM serves lunch and nothing else.

Ways to Save

  • Children 3 and under eat free with a paying adult, commonly capped at two per adult buffet
  • Seniors get $1 to $2 off, and some stores run an early bird rate that throws in a drink
  • Golden Corral Rewards is free, earns a point per dollar and pays out $10 off at 100 points
  • Rotating value deals run all year, so check the offers page the morning of your visit
  • The $2.50 kids weekend breakfast promotion ended in late July, so don't plan around it

Can You Take Leftovers From Golden Corral?

Not from your plate. The all you can eat price covers food eaten on the premises, and staff will clear a half finished plate rather than box it. Ask the cashier for a container before you start instead, then fill it in one trip and pay by weight.

What you packApproximate cost
1 lb, a light meal$8.49 to $8.99
1.5 lb, a hearty meal$12.75 to $13.50
2 lb, a full container$17 to $18

That's the Weigh and Pay program, and it runs as a separate transaction. Drinks and soft serve are usually excluded, and you can't fill a box then settle into a booth. A packed container costs about the same as one buffet, so treat it as convenience rather than a saving.

Breakfast is the cheapest and quietest of the three, which makes it the easy call with young kids in tow. Lunch adds the burger bar for a couple of dollars more. Come back for dinner if you want steak carved in front of you, though Friday to Sunday evenings carry the highest prices of the week.

Final Thoughts

Breakfast at Golden Corral rewards a little planning and punishes a guess. Confirm the hours, expect somewhere between $11 and $13 for adults, budget for drinks, and head for the omelet station before the scrambled eggs draw a crowd. Get the timing right and the Golden Corral breakfast menu delivers one of the best value mornings you'll find for the price of a fast food combo.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Golden Corral still serve breakfast? +
Yes, but mostly on weekends. A small number of stores near highways or tourist areas open midweek. Most suburban locations unlock at 10:30 or 11 AM and head straight to lunch.
What time does Golden Corral stop serving breakfast? +
About 11:00 AM at nearly every location, with a few weekend stores stretching to 11:30. There's no all day breakfast once the switch happens.
Do kids eat free at Golden Corral? +
Children 3 and under eat free with a paying adult, usually two per adult buffet. Older kids pay a reduced rate unless a promotion is running.
Is breakfast included in the buffet price? +
Yes. One price covers the omelet station, hot line, cold bar and sweets. Drinks are often extra, so ask at the register.
Can I get breakfast to go? +
At many stores, yes. Fill a Weigh and Pay container from the morning line and pay by the pound.
Are prices the same at every location? +
No. Each franchise sets its own board, so the same breakfast can run $10.49 in one town and $13.99 in another.
